# Pre-Application Trial and Pilot Gate

Status: **READY FOR HUMAN TRIAL; NOT YET RUN**.

This plan tests whether two people can use the frozen neutral operating method without live coaching. It is separate from the Matt Pocock source audit and does not alter any running project.

## Trial setup

Person A is the owner and contributor. Person B is the independent reviewer and must not produce or edit any reviewed PDF. A records work items and owner/contributor transitions; B records review verdicts and acceptance. A and B receive the method, its blank minimum work record, and only the participant prompts below. Keep the observer key hidden until all cards are finished. A third person may reveal timed prompts and note questions, but must not coach, contribute to a PDF, or review it. With no third person, A and B open sealed prompts at the stated times and score the records afterward.

Prepare three separate work IDs and records, a one-page PDF seed missing its version/date for Card 2, and two sealed Card 3 prompts: “The fictional regular hours are 10:00–18:00” and “A new requirement is separate holiday hours of 11:00–15:00.” Each submission is one PDF; retain earlier versions within the same card. Start every card at DEFINED. Cards 2 and 3 are independent cases, not continuations of Card 1's accepted item.

Use role labels and fictional content, with no private personal details. Agree in advance to retain each completed minimum record, transition and review evidence, rejected output, and accepted PDF snapshots in a restricted or redacted form. If the participants decline that minimum retention, record an anonymized INCONCLUSIVE disposition and stop. Do not score the trial PASS.

Allow up to 60 minutes without coaching and record the actual time. The participants must use the method to choose their own states and verdicts. The observer may record questions but cannot answer them during a card.

## Common participant brief

Create a one-page visitor guide for a fictional shared workroom. It must have a title, opening hours, three visitor rules, a placeholder contact, and a version/date. Use fictional information only. The decision is: “Can a visitor follow this guide without asking staff for the basic rules?” The initial acceptance criteria are one page, all five required elements present, readable at normal zoom, no real personal data, and a PDF that opens. A supplies any unspecified fictional details.

### Participant Card 1 — routine result

There is no uncertain rule to test. A defines and makes the guide. B reviews its fidelity, quality/standards, and operational readiness. Record the result and the next owner/action.

### Participant Card 2 — rejected first submission

Begin a new work item with the common brief and the same initial acceptance criteria. For the first review, A submits the prepared PDF seed as the first output without editing it; B has not been told which element it lacks. If the review does not accept it, A produces a revised PDF under the same work ID and asks B to review again. Retain both submissions and decisions.

### Participant Card 3 — missing input and changed requirement

Begin another new work item. The regular opening hours needed for the PDF have not yet been supplied. A and B proceed as far as the method permits. After A records the missing input, reveal the first sealed prompt and continue to a reviewed result. Only after acceptance, reveal the second sealed prompt adding holiday opening hours. A and B handle the change through the method and produce a reviewed revised PDF. Retain the first accepted version and the later version.

These cards are prompts for observing use. They are not evidence that any transition occurred until completed records and outputs exist.

## Record the observations and disposition

| Measure | Record |
|---|---|
| Participants and roles | A and B, with B independent of every reviewed output |
| Start/end time | Actual time used |
| State routes | Every transition with actor, time, reason, evidence, and baseline |
| Prototype decision | Explicit no-prototype reason or question/result |
| Review | Three separate verdicts and evidence per submission |
| Defects | Missing fields, illegal state, unauthorized acceptance, silent baseline edit, lost rejection/snapshot |
| Questions | Exact terms participants could not interpret without help |
| Outcome | PASS, REVISE, or INCONCLUSIVE with reviewer and date |

PASS requires all three complete retained minimum records and required PDF/evidence snapshots, legal fully recorded transitions, three evidenced verdicts per submission, no self-acceptance or skipped review, and an identifiable current owner and next action for each card. An omission or wrong/unauthorized transition by participants is REVISE. A stopped trial, missing fixture, or refusal to retain minimum redacted evidence is INCONCLUSIVE. Time and questions guide simplification; they are not hidden.

If the trial exposes a method defect, preserve v1.0-static-reviewed as the historical baseline, issue a revised method version with fresh static review, and repeat the affected cards. If the script or fixture caused the problem, correct this plan and rerun the affected cards. Do not relabel synthetic desk checks as human validation.
